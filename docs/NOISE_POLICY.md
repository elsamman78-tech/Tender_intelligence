# Discovery Noise Policy

## Hard rejects from opportunity promotion
The following must never be promoted to the tender dashboard:
- personal LinkedIn profiles
- social-media profiles or posts
- generic news articles
- jobs / careers
- biographies / people pages
- commentary / opinion / press coverage without an actual procurement notice
- award-only notices
- expired or cancelled notices

## Newspaper exception
A newspaper/e-paper page is eligible only when it contains an actual procurement advertisement or tender notice with procurement evidence such as procuring entity, notice type, reference, submission instructions, or deadline.

A press story about a future project is an EARLY_SIGNAL only and remains outside the tender dashboard until an official or advertisement-grade procurement notice is verified.
